Fabrication notes -- picowatt carrier board
===========================================

Board outline : 92.0 x 70.0 mm, plain rectangle, no cutouts, slots or
                V-scoring. Outline is on the Edge_Cuts layer.
Layers        : 2 (FR-4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz finished copper)
Min track     : 0.30 mm (11.8 mil)
Min spacing   : 0.20 mm (7.9 mil)
Min drill     : 0.40 mm (vias)
Min annular   : 0.20 mm (vias), 0.30 mm (component pads)


IMPORTANT -- the 12 mounting holes must be NON-PLATED (NPTH)
------------------------------------------------------------
They are supplied in a separate drill file, picowatt-carrier-NPTH.drl:

    8 x 2.8 mm   standoffs under the daughterboards
    4 x 3.2 mm   board mounting feet

Plating these would connect the mounting screws to the board ground.

All remaining holes are plated (picowatt-carrier-PTH.drl), 83 in total:

    8 x 0.4 mm   vias
   12 x 0.8 mm   resistors and capacitors
   61 x 1.0 mm   pin sockets and test point
    2 x 1.6 mm   screw terminal


Other
-----
No SMD parts on this board, so no solder paste stencil is required.
Surface finish: HASL is fine, all assembly is through-hole hand soldering.

-----

Drill maps     : *-drl_map.gbr
